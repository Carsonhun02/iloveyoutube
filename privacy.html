Privacy policy

iloveyoutube is a personal organizer used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube API Services. Every action is chosen and approved by the owner.

When the owner signs in a channel, the tool can access that channel's:

  • videos and their details (title, description, thumbnail, visibility and publish time), so the owner can upload and edit them;
  • playlists, so the owner can organize videos into them;
  • statistics and analytics, so the owner can see how the channel is doing;
  • comments, so the owner can read and reply to them.

It does not collect, store or share data about anyone else. Sign-in tokens stay on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

iloveyoutube uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
