Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how iloveyoutube (the "App") handles information. The App is a personal organizer used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube API Services. Every action in the App is chosen and approved by the owner.

Information the App accesses

When the owner signs in one of their channels with Google, the App can access the following information for that channel:

  • Videos and video details, such as titles, descriptions, thumbnails, visibility and publish times, so the owner can upload and edit videos.
  • Playlists, so the owner can organize videos into them.
  • Statistics and analytics, so the owner can see how the channel is performing.
  • Comments, so the owner can read and reply to them.

How the information is used

The information is used only to show the owner their own channels and to carry out the actions the owner selects. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens and channel information are stored only on the owner's own computer. The App does not collect, sell or share information with anyone, and it does not collect information about other people.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube and Google

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App, the owner is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
