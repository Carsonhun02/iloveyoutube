iloveyoutube

A personal organizer for YouTube channels

iloveyoutube (the "App") is a personal tool that brings a creator's own YouTube channels together in one place. It keeps videos, playlists, publishing schedules, channel statistics and comments organized so the channels are easier to manage.

How it works

  • The owner signs in each of their own channels with Google.
  • Videos, playlists, statistics and comments are shown in one organized view.
  • Every upload, edit and reply is chosen and approved by the owner. Nothing is posted on its own.

Who it is for

The App is used only by its owner to manage the owner's own channels. It is not offered to the public.
